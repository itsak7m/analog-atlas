Analog Atlas · theme previews

Early design draft, kept to show how the look was chosen (theme A became the app). It is not the app and it is not updated. The mock ranking uses the brief's section 6 reference climate match for Jezero (NASA POWER), for the brief's sites that are still in the app; Mauna Kea was later removed from the app. For current scores, open the app. Field-kit values are labelled Simulated. Nothing here can be clicked: this page only shows the look.

A Mission Control

A NASA flight-control console at night: calm, exact, telemetry everywhere. Apollo-era amber numbers on a modern glass cockpit.

  • #05080F void
  • #0E1626 panel
  • #1A2640 grid
  • #FFB547 telemetry amber
  • #5CE1E6 live cyan
  • #E6EDF7 text
Fonts
IBM Plex Sans Condensed (labels) · IBM Plex Sans (text) · IBM Plex Mono (numbers)
Signature
Corner brackets, navigation grid, uppercase telemetry labels. Amber = scores, cyan = live sensor data.
Fly-in
Altitude, latitude and longitude read-outs plus a horizon line while the camera descends.

B Basalt & Sandstone

An expedition atlas and a geologist's field notebook: warm, tactile and rooted in Jordan's rocks. Black basalt, red Wadi Rum sandstone, ochre dust.

  • #13110F basalt
  • #1B1815 panel
  • #D9653F Wadi Rum red
  • #E6AD5C ochre
  • #A8A29E regolith
  • #F3E9DC paper
Fonts
Fraunces (headings) · Archivo (text) · JetBrains Mono (numbers)
Signature
Topographic contour lines, specimen labels, stamped badges (CANDIDATE, NASA ANALOG), rock-type colour chips.
Fly-in
Field-note captions for elevation, rock type and slope as the ground comes closer.

C Petra Night

The night sky over Wadi Rum: deep indigo, starlight and gold. Cinematic and premium. Earth twins are joined to their Moon or Mars target by gold constellation lines.

  • #070818 night
  • #16183A glass
  • #F2C36B gold
  • #E8A598 Petra rose
  • #9097BD dusk
  • #E9EBF7 moonlight
Fonts
Marcellus SC (headings, carved-stone feel) · Manrope (text) · DM Mono (numbers)
Signature
Star field, frosted-glass panels, gold arcs linking twins to the target, eight-point star mark.
Fly-in
Slow cinematic descent with gold title cards.

Earth image: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation (Reto Stöckli), public domain, from the app's textures/earth.jpg. Fonts: IBM Plex from the app's vendor/fonts; the fonts of themes B and C come from Google Fonts and need the internet (all SIL Open Font License).
